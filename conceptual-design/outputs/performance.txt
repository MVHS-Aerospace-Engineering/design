 8. WATCH-OUTS
======================================================================================
  1. 'elevator_authority' is at its limit (margin 1.0%) - it sized the design; a heavier build or measured inputs may push it over.
  2. 'spiral' is at its limit (margin 0.0%) - it sized the design; a heavier build or measured inputs may push it over.
  3. 'endurance' is at its limit (margin 0.1%) - it sized the design; a heavier build or measured inputs may push it over.
  4. At loiter speed (11.5 m/s, only 1.20 x the stall speed) the wing stalls in a level turn steeper than ~46 deg of bank - limit the autopilot's bank angle in loiter or add speed for turns.
  5. Weathercock stability is modest (Cn_beta 0.044/rad): expect the nose to wander in gusts; this is why the fin is never allowed below the Raymer tail-volume size.
