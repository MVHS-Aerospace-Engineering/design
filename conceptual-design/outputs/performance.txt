 8. WATCH-OUTS
======================================================================================
  1. Approach trim at the FORWARD CG needs -16.6 deg of elevator at a tail angle of -10.2 deg - outside the range NeuralFoil trusts (tail near its negative stall). Balance toward the aft half of the CG range, and check elevator authority on the first approaches.
  2. 'elevator_authority' is at its limit (margin 1.0%) - it sized the design; a heavier build or measured inputs may push it over.
  3. At loiter speed (11.8 m/s, only 1.20 x the stall speed) the wing stalls in a level turn steeper than ~46 deg of bank - limit the autopilot's bank angle in loiter or add speed for turns.
  4. Weathercock stability is modest (Cn_beta 0.058/rad): expect the nose to wander in gusts; this is why the fin is never allowed below the Raymer tail-volume size.
